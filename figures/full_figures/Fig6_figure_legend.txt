Fig. 6 | Predicted substrate utilization and experimental growth of three anaerobic isolates. a, Fractions of 213 MAG models supporting growth before and after MQC for the 12 most widely utilized substrates in a 52-substrate survey. b, Mean relative-growth agreement for KZ1544, JCM6485 and JCM9990, each tested on nine substrates. Agreement is 1 - |p - y| for normalized predicted and experimental values; the overall score averages all 27 pairs. Right-hand values show post-minus-pre changes. c-e, Signed deviations (predicted minus experimental relative growth) for KZ1544, JCM6485 and JCM9990. Open blue and filled orange circles represent initial and corrected predictions; connecting lines pair stages, and zero denotes agreement. Each experimental or model profile is normalized to its within-strain maximum across nine substrates. Asterisks mark three retained JCM9990 growth-rate fits with R² < 0.8.
