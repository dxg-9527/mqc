Fig. 3 | Energy, reducing-equivalent and biomass outputs before and after correction. a,b, ATP and NADH ECDFs for four model collections. Solid lines show initial maximum-production fluxes (mmol gDCW⁻¹ h⁻¹); dashed lines show final yields normalized to carbon influx (mol per carbon-mol). The asterisk denotes this difference in normalization. Per-stage sample sizes are 5,584 CarveMe, 73 BiGG, 79 ModelSEED and 794 VMH models. c, Biomass outputs under the input carbon-supply configuration (initial), after carbon-supply restriction (intermediate), and after completion of reaction and biomass curation (final). Violin plots include all available values, with up to 700 points overlaid per collection; boxes show the interquartile range and median of displayed values. Display ranges are 0-230, 0-2.0 and 0-2.2 h⁻¹. d, Initial and final biomass ECDFs for CarveMe and VMH, followed by final medians and percentages within 0-1 h⁻¹ for all four collections. Biomass sample sizes are 5,533, 66, 79 and 763 per stage, respectively. The green interval is a descriptive reference; the pale 80-100% band is a visual guide. Values below 10⁻³ are placed at 10⁻³ on logarithmic axes; numerical summaries use unmodified values.
